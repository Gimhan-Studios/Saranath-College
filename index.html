Our History

A Journey Through Time

The milestones that shaped our College — from a humble beginning to the institution we are proud of today.

1882

The Beginning

The College was founded with 30 students under the leadership of Headmaster Anthony de Silva.

1924

A Bilingual School

The school became a bilingual institution, widening the doors of learning.

1941

Growing Strong

Enrolment grew to more than 500 students.

1944

Three Languages, One School

The College became a mixed Sinhala, Tamil and English school — a proud symbol of unity.

1947

A Central College

Under Dr. C.W.W. Kannangara's free education policy, the school was elevated to a Central College.

1951 July 7

A New Chapter

The Central College was relocated to Meegahakotuwa, and the Sinhala, English and Tamil mixed school continued here as before.

1977

Leadership

Mr. H.M. Dayananda Herath served as Principal of the College.

2017

Academic Excellence

124 students gained admission to state universities — a landmark result for the College.

2021 June 14

A New Era of Development

Mr. R.M.C. Randeniya assumed duties as Principal for the second time, leading major academic and infrastructure development.

Today

On the World Stage

Saranath students continue to win international honours in sports and co-curricular activities.

From the Principal

Principal's Message

Mr. R.M.C. Randeniya
Principal, Saranath College

“Every great institution is built by those who came before us. Ours began with thirty students and a vision.”

It is with immense pride that I welcome you to Saranath College, Kuliyapitiya. Founded in 1882 under the leadership of Headmaster Anthony de Silva with just thirty students, our College has grown over more than a century into a place that has shaped thousands of lives.

Our history is one of courage and unity. We became a bilingual school in 1924, welcomed over 500 students by 1941, and in 1944 opened our doors to Sinhala, Tamil and English-medium students alike. In 1947, under Dr. C.W.W. Kannangara's vision of free education, we became a Central College — and ever since, Saranath has carried that spirit of opportunity for every child.

We take pride not only in our past but in what our students achieve today. In 2017, 124 of our students earned university admission. Today, Saranath students bring home international honours in sports and co-curricular activities — a testament to their talent, discipline and the dedication of our teachers.

Since resuming duties as Principal in June 2021, it has been my privilege to lead the College through a period of significant academic and physical development, together with our committed staff, parents, past pupils and well-wishers.

Our motto, පඤ්ඤා නරානං රතනං, reminds us that wisdom is the true jewel of humankind. I invite every Saranathian, past and present, to carry that jewel with pride, and to keep our College shining for generations to come.

R.M.C. RandeniyaPrincipal · Saranath College, Kuliyapitiya
